## Approach 2: Minimal Brutalist
<response>
<text>
**Design Movement**: Soft Brutalism
**Core Principles**: Strong typographic hierarchy, raw borders, asymmetric layout, deliberate contrast
**Color Philosophy**: Off-white canvas, jet black text, electric yellow accent — bold and unapologetic
**Layout Paradigm**: Left-heavy asymmetric grid; navigation pinned left sidebar on desktop
**Signature Elements**: Thick black outlines on cards, oversized section numbers, monospaced labels
**Interaction Philosophy**: Instant state changes, no easing — snappy and decisive
**Animation**: Minimal; only opacity transitions at 100ms
**Typography System**: "Space Grotesk" headlines, "IBM Plex Mono" for labels/badges
</text>

## Selected Approach: **Approach 1 — Playful Precision**

This approach most faithfully matches the original SpinPick aesthetic: clean white/near-white background, vivid purple accent, rounded buttons, and a prominent colorful wheel. It balances professionalism with approachable playfulness — exactly what a decision-making tool needs.
